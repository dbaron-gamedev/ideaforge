# Game Development Project Brief

## Objective

Identify commercially promising independent
video game concepts.

## Constraints

- Platform: PC / Steam
- Team size: 5 developers
- Development duration: 12 months
- Budget: $300,000 CAD
- Engine: Unity
- Business model: Premium
- Target market: Global

## Requirements

- Generate five distinct game concepts.
- Prioritize innovative gameplay mechanics.
- Avoid excessive production complexity.
- Avoid expensive multiplayer infrastructure.

## Market Research

Prioritize market data from the last 12 months.
